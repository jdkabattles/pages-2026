Referral Bonus Checklist: Check the Terms Before You Miss Them

A referral bonus is not earned by sharing a link alone. It is earned by meeting every qualifying step in the current terms, in the right order, before the deadline. This checklist helps you read those terms carefully. It does not promise any payout, and amounts, if any, vary by active offer — always check the offer you actually joined.

Brands named here are examples only. Cash App, Ibotta, Chime, and Muse are named because they are the referral topics Jonathan has written about. This page is not affiliated with any of them, and no referral code is published on this page.

1. Find the current terms, not a summary

Open the official terms or help page for the exact offer. Screenshots, blog summaries, and older posts can be out of date. If the terms and a summary disagree, the official terms govern.

2. Who qualifies — and who does not

Check whether the offer is for new customers only, whether the referred person must meet a condition, and whether either side must already hold an account. Do not assume a friend, a family member, or a second account qualifies.

3. The qualifying steps, in order

List each required step in order, for example: sign up, verify identity, add a payment method, make a qualifying deposit or purchase, or activate a card. Note which steps must happen before others, and whether a step must be completed within a set number of days after signing up or after the qualifying event.

4. Card activation and deposit rules

Some offers require a physical card to be activated, or a qualifying direct deposit or purchase of a minimum size. Check the exact wording: a deposit that is too small, too late, or of the wrong type may not count, even if money moved.

5. Deadlines and caps

Write down every deadline: to sign up, to complete the qualifying step, and to receive the reward. Check for a cap on how many referrals can be rewarded in a year or in total. A bonus that is capped or expired is not a bonus you missed by bad luck — it is a term you can read in advance.

6. Keep proof, and wait for the stated window

Save the terms you read, the date you completed each step, and any confirmation you received. Rewards often arrive within a stated window after qualification, not instantly. If the window passes, contact the provider through its official support route — do not share passwords, account details, or codes with anyone claiming to help.
